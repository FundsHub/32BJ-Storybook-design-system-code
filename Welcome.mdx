# 32BJ Public Site Design System

**V1.1 — Responsive + Functional Coverage**

This Storybook starter is aligned to the approved 32BJ Figma system and covers the 22 documented component areas.

## Fund themes

- Health — `#0077C8`
- Training — `#398532`
- Retirement / Pension — `#C94B1F`
- Legal — `#FFBF0F`

## Implementation rules

- Keep global alerts separate from page-level callouts.
- Preserve source-backed Fund navigation and nested IA.
- Use 44px minimum touch targets on mobile.
- Treat 390px as a review viewport, not a hard device breakpoint.
- Keep Legal dark text on yellow surfaces.
- Keep body text neutral on light surfaces except approved Fund accent roles.
- Open Accordion content must grow with its content.
- Placeholder text is never the only accessible label.
